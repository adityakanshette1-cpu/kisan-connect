Karnataka Government Farmer Schemes

Stay informed with official financial support and procurement schemes explained simply:

Raitha Siri Scheme

Provides ₹10,000 per hectare by direct benefit transfer (DBT) for farmers cultivating minor millets such as Ragi and Jowar to encourage sustainable nutrition.

MSP Procurement Support

Government approved procurement for Kharif crops including sunflower, green gram, and soybean at Minimum Support Price (MSP) with direct payments credited within 3 days.

Totapuri Mango Price Support

Special price support and procurement programs facilitating bulk purchase at notified APMC mandis across Kolar and Chikkaballapur regions.

FRUITS ID Portal Registration

Unified Farmer Registration (FRUITS) system needed to seamlessly access CM Raitha Vidya Nidhi scholarships, crop loans, and input subsidies.
